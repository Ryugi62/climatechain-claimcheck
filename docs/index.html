ClaimCheck

Paste one carbon-offset sentence from an ESG report. We check the credit serial in it against public Gold Standard retirement records, and an on-chain registry refuses to count the same credits twice.

Data note: lookups use a stored sample of 140 real registry records (fetched 2026-10-17). Tick "live lookup" to ask the registry's public read endpoint for your own serial. No wallet, no login. Chain part: the registry contract is deployed on the Sepolia testnet (below).

Wording check (helper, never changes the verdict)

Compares the words around the serial (project type, country, year) with the registry record using a small AI model that runs in your browser. Result: Backed, May conflict or No receipt. It can miss changed facts; the numbers are under "Measured, not promised". First use downloads about 90 MB once from huggingface.co.
